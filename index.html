About the Game

Whack-a-Mole is a simple arcade game. Moles randomly appear from holes. Your goal is to hit as many moles as possible before the time runs out.

How to Play

1

Click Start Game to begin.

2

Click on the moles when they appear.

3

Get as many points as possible before the timer reaches 0!
